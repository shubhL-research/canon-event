## The break, as observed

Not staged. The arm was built from a search URL and ran clean against search
pages. Feeding it a product URL produced:

    error_code        wait_element_timeout
    message           waiting for selector ".s-main-slot" failed:
                      timeout 30000ms exceeded
    input             https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0CN8XJB15

`.s-main-slot` is Amazon's search-results grid. It does not exist on a product
page, so the collector waited thirty seconds for an element that was never
coming and failed every product page it was given.

This matters more than a missing selector. Identity re-assertion is only valid
against the product page itself, because Amazon substitutes ASINs on stale URLs.
An arm that cannot open a product page cannot make a RED claim at all.

## What was asked for

> On product detail pages the scraper waits for the search-results selector
> .s-main-slot, which only exists on search pages, so every product page fails
> with wait_element_timeout. Detect the page type: on a /dp/ product page wait
> for the product title element instead, and extract title, brand, price,
> currency, availability text, add-to-cart button text, seller and the
> manufacturer part number or EAN from the product details table.

21 steps, five `code_fixer` iterations, stopped at the approval gate.

## What the canary found

**The reported fault was genuinely fixed.** The proposed template opens a product
page and returns title, price, currency, part number, seller and buy control. On
the narrow question asked, the heal succeeded.

It was refused on two things the prompt did not ask about, both visible in the
single preview row:

**1. `ean` returns the review star rating.**

    ean   "4.1 4.1 ud af 5 stjerner (50) 4.1 ud af 5 stjerner"

That is not a barcode. GTIN is the stronger of the two identifiers this project
can assert on, and it is the one preferred when a notice carries both. A template
that fills the GTIN field with review furniture does not merely lose the GTIN
path, it fills it with digits, and digits are what the matcher is looking for.

The check-digit guard in `collect/adjudicate.py` rejects the string, so no false
RED could have reached the wall. The guard holding is not a reason to ship the
template; it is the reason nothing worse happened.

**2. `brand` returns the storefront link.**

    brand   "Ga til Comfyer butikken"

The "visit the store" anchor, not the manufacturer.

## The finding that outlived the heal

The preview row came back in **Danish**, from `amazon.de`:

    availability_text        "På lager"
    add_to_cart_button_text  "Tilføj til indkøbskurv"
    brand                    "Gå til Comfyer butikken"
    price.currency           "EUR"

The currency is correct. The page is not German.

This falsifies an assumption the project was carrying: that the storefront's
currency symbol proves which market answered. It does not. `amazon.de` quotes EUR
to every visitor from every exit, so **EUR on the DE arm is necessary and not
sufficient**. A Danish exit produces a page that passes a currency check and is
not a German listing.

The buy-control check caught it, but by luck rather than by design: Danish
"Tilføj til indkøbskurv" is not in the German label list, so no buy control was
found and no RED row could form. Luck is not a control.

Consequences, applied:

- **Page language is the geo attestation. Currency is corroboration.** The order
  is reversed from what the plan assumed.
- The re-heal adds a `page_language` field reading the `html` element's `lang`
  attribute, so the locale is recorded from inside the page rather than inferred
  from the wording of a button.
- `README.md` needs this under weaknesses. It argues against a measure we were
  relying on, which is the reason it goes in the open rather than in a footnote.

## Why refusing was correct

The gate is two-sided by design: a repair has to fix the fault AND leave the
evidence chain intact. This one did the first and damaged the second. Approving
it would have restored the arm to working order while quietly filling the GTIN
field with review text — an arm that looks healthier than before and is less
trustworthy than before, which is the specific outcome the gate exists to
prevent.

Production template unchanged. Arm DE remains WITHHELD. The wall says so.
